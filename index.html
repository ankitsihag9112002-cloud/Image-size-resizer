Support & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the compressed file ever exceed my chosen KB size (e.g., 50.1 KB when set to 50 KB)? ▼

No. If you select 50 KB, our binary-search byte engine locks a hard upper ceiling at 50.0 KB (51,200 bytes). It verifies the exact output byte count before finishing so the result is always strictly at or below your chosen KB size—never 50.1 KB.

How does this tool prevent image quality drop and cropping? ▼

Zero cropping is ever performed—your full image frame and aspect ratio remain 100% intact. To prevent blurriness or compression blocks, our engine enforces a high JPEG quality floor (72%–98%) and uses multi-step high-smoothing canvas rendering so facial features and ink strokes stay razor-sharp.

How does the Signature Compressor mode differ from the Image mode? ▼

Signature mode flattens transparent PNG backgrounds onto a crisp pure-white canvas and preserves high-contrast ink strokes, making it effortless to hit tight 10 KB – 20 KB limits without blurry handwriting.

Are my photos or signatures stored on any server? ▼

Never. Every calculation uses your device's built-in HTML5 Canvas and Web APIs. Your images never leave your phone or computer.
