Frequently Asked Questions

Will the compressed file ever exceed my chosen KB size?▼

No. Our binary-search byte engine locks a hard upper ceiling at your exact target KB so the result is always strictly at or below your chosen limit.

Does this tool crop my image or reduce clarity?▼

Zero cropping is ever performed. Your full image frame and aspect ratio remain 100% intact with high-clarity canvas rendering.

Are my photos stored on any server?▼

Never. Every calculation uses your device's browser memory locally.

Which file format do I get?▼

You can upload JPG, PNG or WEBP. The downloaded file is always a JPG, which is what most application portals accept.

My form needs exact pixel dimensions. Can this help?▼

This tool controls file size in KB. It keeps your original proportions and does not set exact width and height, so resize first if your form requires specific dimensions.

Why is the result a little smaller than my limit?▼

A small safety margin is kept under your limit so portals that count size slightly differently still accept the file.
